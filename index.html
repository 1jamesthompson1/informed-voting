The survey

Answer for yourself. There are no right answers.

Use the five buttons under each statement. “Unsure” is a real answer — it will be treated as neutral. You can skip any statement, change your mind, and come back later: your answers are saved on this device only.

◉
Why the party names are hidden

Research on voting and decision-making shows that party labels can change how people judge the same policy. Hiding the names reduces that pull. The names, positions and sources are all revealed after you finish — and on the audit page at any time.
